## Next item being tackled here

The sampling measure is a concrete unresolved part of the numerical index. A cyclic clock can establish uniform visitation of its own phase states, but that does not automatically establish independence of several sampled labels. The next formalization will distinguish:

1. Empirical frequencies derived from a full cyclic orbit.
2. Uniform marginals versus a uniform joint product distribution.
3. The event denominators available from a finite deterministic cycle.
4. What additional dynamics or weights would be required to realize the specified Toll and Margin probabilities.

Any result here will be stated for its actual dynamical hypotheses. It will not be presented as fixing the vacuum law merely because a candidate sampler can be constructed.
