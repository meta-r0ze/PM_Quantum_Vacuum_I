\section{Chirality: Remaining Dynamical Requirements}
\label{app:geometric_origin_of_chiral_fermions}
The $D_4$ channel bookkeeping does not yet construct the observed chiral fermions. Distler and Garibaldi analyze candidate embeddings into real and complex forms of $E_8$ and obtain representation-theoretic obstructions to the required chiral gauge theory\cite{distler_there_2010}. Their result is not restricted to an everywhere Euclidean signature. Assigning an effective Lorentzian metric or starting from a discrete lattice does not by itself demonstrate an evasion of their hypotheses.

In particular, an internal Euclidean geometry does not imply absence of a Hamiltonian: internal degrees of freedom can evolve with respect to the spacetime time coordinate. Treating some channels as static at quiescent equilibrium is an approximation or model restriction, not a proof that mirror states have zero propagating capacity.

A physical derivation must specify the microscopic state space and update, derive a low-energy fermion operator, identify its gauge representations and chirality, and explain the fate of any mirror modes while preserving the required conservation laws. It must then state precisely which hypotheses of the embedding obstruction apply. These dynamical tasks remain open and are not implied by the present Lean results.
