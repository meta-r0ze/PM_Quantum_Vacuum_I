\begin{abstract}
Persistence Mechanics (PM) holds that any system that persists must satisfy Finiteness, Conservation, and Causality, realized through six pillars: Capacity, Map, Protocol, Governor, Toll, and Margin. We apply it to the quantum vacuum, treated as a closed system with zero external flux. Writing PM's requirements as explicit postulates on a discrete substrate, we prove in Lean~4 that they admit exactly one substrate up to isometry: the $E_8$ lattice, with two orthogonal $D_4$ sectors and a 43-state clock. Every integer that enters the cost of persistence is then forced. Reading each pillar as an expected number of transitions per cycle gives the structural index
$Z_{\rm geo}=43\pi+2-\tfrac{1}{167}-\tfrac{2}{43}+\tfrac{334}{32^3\cdot5\cdot172}+\tfrac{1}{31\cdot6\cdot43^2}=\num{\AlphaInvVal}\ldots$,
certified to eighteen decimal places, with no continuous parameter. Under an electromagnetic matching condition $Z_{\rm geo}=\alpha^{-1}(0)$, it agrees with Morel et al.\ (2020) within $0.58\,\sigma$ and CODATA 2022 within $1.68\,\sigma$, and lies $3.1\,\sigma$ and $6.2\,\sigma$ from the Fan (2023) and Parker (2018) determinations. The proofs use only Lean's standard axioms; their premises are the stated postulates and PM's definitions of the pillars. The matching condition is a separate physical hypothesis.
\end{abstract}
