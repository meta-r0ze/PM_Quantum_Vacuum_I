\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We applied Persistence Mechanics to the vacuum as a closed system and asked whether the requirements of persistence determine its structure. Written as explicit postulates, they do. Finiteness, readability of the substrate's own distinctions, bit-addressed glue, local exchange, and economy admit exactly one substrate up to isometry, the $E_8$ lattice. Its sectors, channels, signature, boundary, and clock then fix every integer that enters the cost of persistence: $D=4$, $\Delta=43$, $\nu=16$, $N=32$, $\sigma=5$, $\chi=2$. Reading the six pillars as expected transitions per cycle gives the index $Z_{\rm geo}=137.035999212\ldots$ with no continuous parameter. Lean checks every step from the postulates to the certified value, using only its standard axioms.

What is proved is an implication. Its premises are the postulates and PM's definitions of the pillars, each stated where it is used, and the postulates are shown to be consistent and independent. Under the further hypothesis that the substrate's net impedance is the infrared Maxwell coefficient, $Z_{\rm geo}=\alpha^{-1}(0)$, in agreement with the kinematic determination of Morel et al.\ within $0.58\,\sigma$ and CODATA 2022 within $1.68\,\sigma$.

Three tasks follow. First, derive the matching condition by computing the substrate's response to a background $U(1)$ field. Second, give the Quantization Gap a quantitative form: a correction to a named high-loop observable, with sign and size. Third, build on the same unadjusted substrate the dynamics it was not asked to supply here: a chiral matter spectrum consistent with the known embedding obstructions, a continuum limit, and the remaining constants of the Standard Model. Each is a test the substrate can fail.

If these results hold, physical constants are not free parameters but properties of the least costly structure that can persist on its own. By PM's selection principle, any persistent system solves the same architectural problem; the vacuum is the case with nothing outside it.
