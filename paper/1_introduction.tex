\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

The idea that physical reality is constrained by the cost of processing information goes back to Landauer's principle\cite{landauer_irreversibility_1961} and Wheeler's ``It from Bit''\cite{wheeler_information_1989}. Verlinde\cite{verlinde_origin_2011} and Bianconi\cite{bianconi_thermodynamics_2025} have proposed that gravity and spacetime emerge from information gradients. These programs motivate a systems-first question: if physical structure is informationally constrained, what minimal architecture makes that information processing possible at all? Causal sets\cite{bombelli_space-time_1987}, loop quantum gravity\cite{rovelli_quantum_2004}, and emergent gravity\cite{verlinde_origin_2011,jacobson_thermodynamics_1995} start from spacetime or gravity. Here we start from persistence.

\textit{Persistence Mechanics}\cite{kate_meyer_persistence_2026} (PM) describes what any system must instantiate to remain identifiable through time. Its three requirements are Finiteness (bounded resources), Conservation (preserved identity-defining distinctions), and Causality (well-ordered evolution). They are realized by six pillars: Capacity and Margin (Finiteness), Map and Protocol (Conservation), Toll and Governor (Causality). Operating the pillars costs transitions, and histories that cost more are exponentially less likely to survive.

PM is usually applied to systems that persist inside an environment: cells, networks, organizations. We ask how far down it reaches. The quantum vacuum is the limiting case: a persistent system with zero external flux, no deeper substrate, and nothing outside it to supply memory, a clock, or a unit of measure. If PM's requirements, applied to such a system, single out its structure, the vacuum is the baseline instance of persistence. If they do not, the vacuum's structure is set by something PM does not describe.

\subsection{Result}
We formalize PM's requirements for the vacuum as explicit postulates on a discrete substrate (\Cref{tab:postulates}) and prove the consequences in Lean~4. The postulates admit exactly one substrate up to isometry, the $E_8$ lattice (\Cref{sec:System0_vacuum_requirements}). It carries two orthogonal $D_4$ sectors, $16$ channel coordinates per sector, one time direction, a spherical defect boundary, and a clock with $43$ states (\Cref{sec:system1_projection}). Every integer that enters the cost of persistence is forced.

Each pillar's cost is an expected number of transitions per cycle. On the forced substrate the six terms sum to the structural index (\Cref{sec:system2_geometric_impedance})
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:intro_index}
Z_{\rm geo}=43\pi+2-\frac{1}{167}-\frac{2}{43}+\frac{334}{32^3\cdot5\cdot172}+\frac{1}{31\cdot6\cdot43^2}.
\end{equation}
Lean certifies $137.035999212381397211<Z_{\rm geo}<137.035999212381397213$. No continuous parameter enters. Under an electromagnetic matching condition, $Z_{\rm geo}$ is the inverse fine-structure constant $\alpha^{-1}(0)$; we compare it with measurement and draw the consequences in \Cref{sec:empirical_correspondences}.

\subsection{What is proved, and from what}
The main theorem (Lean: \texttt{Main.\allowbreak persistence\_selects}) states: from the postulates of \Cref{tab:postulates}, the substrate is $E_8$ with two $D_4$ sectors, the clock period is $43$, the parameter record is forced, and $Z_{\rm geo}$ is given by \Cref{eq:intro_index} and lies in the certified interval. A second theorem shows that $E_8$, the 43-cycle, one time direction, and the sphere satisfy every postulate, so the set is consistent. Each geometric postulate is independent of the others: for each, Lean exhibits a lattice that meets every other postulate but not that one.

The premises are the postulates themselves and PM's definitions of the pillars, in particular that Governor restricts transitions and Protocol coordinates them. The proofs use only Lean's standard axioms (\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}); a strict audit checks every one of the project's 1236 declarations. The electromagnetic matching condition of \Cref{sec:empirical_correspondences} is not part of the theorem. The Lean project, with a map from each claim to its theorem, is in the \texttt{lean/} directory of the accompanying repository.

\subsection{Scope}
This paper treats the static, quiescent baseline of the vacuum. Dynamics, a Lagrangian, gauge structure, a chiral matter spectrum, and Lorentz invariance of the continuum limit are extensions, not prerequisites for the question asked here. In particular, the Distler--Garibaldi analysis of $E_8$ embeddings\cite{distler_there_2010} constrains any later derivation of chiral matter; we state what such a derivation must establish in \Cref{app:geometric_origin_of_chiral_fermions}.
