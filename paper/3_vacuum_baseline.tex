\section{The Vacuum as a Closed Persistent System}
\label{sec:the_test_case}
We apply PM to the vacuum in its quiescent steady state: stationary operation at zero external flux. The vacuum has no deeper system beneath it and no environment beside it. It cannot obtain configuration data, boundary conditions, synchronization signals, a measuring probe, or a unit of cost from outside itself. We state this as one constraint.
\begin{quote}
\textbf{Closure Constraint.} The vacuum's state space, its update, and everything used to read, time, or price its states belong to the vacuum itself.
\end{quote}
\label{subsec:closure}
Closure is used in three concrete ways below.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item \textbf{Internal probes.} Any readout of the substrate is made by the substrate. A probe is itself a substrate state, and its outcome is held at the substrate's own resolution (\Cref{sec:readability}).
\item \textbf{No preferred label.} Nothing outside can single out one element of a finite set of labels the substrate cannot distinguish internally. Admissible sets are then empty or everything, and the only admissible probability on such a set is uniform (Lean: \texttt{ClosureSymmetry.invariant\_set}, \texttt{invariant\_measure\_uniform}). Where the substrate's own symmetries are known this is checked rather than assumed: the automorphisms of $E_8$ that preserve its two $D_4$ sectors act transitively on the $N=32$ channels, so invariance under them alone forces the uniform measure there (\texttt{ChannelSymmetry.invariant\_uniform}), and the clock advance does the same on the $\Delta$ slots (\texttt{governor\_uniform}). For the $\sigma$ interaction directions (three spatial positions and two boundary points, which are of different kinds), the residual target addresses and Margin's configurations, no such symmetry is established, and the uniform measure on them is this premise.
\item \textbf{One currency.} There is no external unit. Lengths, counts, and probabilities are all measured in elementary transitions, with one transition as the unit of cost.
\end{enumerate}
Conservation is read in PM's sense: the substrate preserves the distinctions that define its identity. On a finite state space with an autonomous update this means the update never merges two states, which is exactly bijectivity (Lean: \texttt{never\_merge\_iff\_bijective}).
